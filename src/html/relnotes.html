Tabli 4.1.0
Oct. 17, 2026
What's New (4.1.0):
  • New option to sort Other Open Windows by most recently used (in the section header or Preferences)
  • Reopened saved windows now open at the size and position of your last browser window
  • Fix windows sometimes failing to open on multi-monitor setups
  • Fix saved window title changes and other edits sometimes being lost
